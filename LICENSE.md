## A note on the profiles

The access profiles and footprints describe what specific distribution
packages install on specific OS versions, captured on the dates recorded in
each file's provenance header. They are evidence and a starting point for
review — **not** a security guarantee for your environment. Review them
against your own footprints before applying anything to a system you care
about.
